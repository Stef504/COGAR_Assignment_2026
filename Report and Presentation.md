## Presentation Structure:
The presentation must include the following elements:
 
- Research Problem  
  Clear definition and explanation of the research problem addressed.
 
- State of the Art  
  Overview of the current state of research related to the topic.
 
- Research Formulation  
  Description of the research conducted, including objectives, hypotheses, and methodology.
 
- Cognitive Approach  
  Explanation of the cognitive approach adopted, with explicit connections to the course Cognitive Architectures for Robotics (COGAR).
 
- Congruence of Results and Conclusions  
  Critical discussion ensuring consistency between the obtained results and the final conclusions.
